Taj Mahal

PP710 · Engineered Quartz by Empirestone

Taj Mahal by Empirestone draws inspiration from one of the world's most coveted natural stones, reimagined as a precision-engineered quartz surface. Its warm cream and soft beige tones, accented by gentle flowing movement, evoke the timeless grandeur of its namesake while delivering the practical benefits of engineered quartz — zero sealing, consistent patterning, and exceptional durability. Perfect for luxury residential kitchens, spa-style bathrooms, and statement hospitality surfaces.
