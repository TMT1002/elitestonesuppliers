Mining Location

Sivas lies at an elevation of 4,183 feet (1,275 meters) in the broad valley of the Kizil River. Sivas contains some of the finest remains of 13th-century Seljuq architecture.

Our quarry is located in the high elevations of Sivas — one of the coldest regions in Türkiye. The production site sits at an altitude of 2,600 meters above sea level, which gives our natural stone exceptional characteristics far above classical marble values.

High abrasion and scratch resistance are among the most important criteria in marble use. Our stones are preferred in large-scale projects such as shopping malls and hospitals. Even marble samples left in sea water are unaffected by salt — a testament to the unmatched durability of our stone.
