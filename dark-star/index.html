Dark Star

Actual Size Varies by Application

Dark Star Marble from Turkey presents dramatic deep tones that enrich sophisticated interiors with bold character. With polished and honed surface options, this marble delivers a striking presence in floors, walls, and design accents. Dark Star's resilience makes it suitable for high-use areas, offering enduring beauty and performance. Natural stone's unique veining and texture ensure that each slab is one-of-a-kind, perfect for designers and homeowners who seek impactful, long-lasting material solutions. Whether creating a modern monolithic look or a high-contrast classic atmosphere, Dark Star Marble stands as a premium choice.
