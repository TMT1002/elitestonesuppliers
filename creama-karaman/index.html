Creama Karaman

Premium Turkish Stone — Warm Beige Tones

Creama Karaman features a distinctive cream-to-beige base with rich, warm veining patterns named after the Karaman region of Türkiye. Its warm, neutral palette — perfect for Mediterranean, classic, modern, and minimalist styles — makes it one of the most sought-after stones for luxury interiors. No two slabs are identical, giving each installation a unique character.
