Misterio Gold

PQ117 · Engineered Quartz by Empirestone

Misterio Gold by Empirestone elevates the classic white quartz aesthetic with threads of warm gold veining that catch the light and add a distinctive sense of luxury. The cream base and luminous gold movement create a surface that feels both refined and warm — an ideal centrepiece for kitchen islands, master bathroom vanities, and feature wall installations. Engineered for perfection, Misterio Gold brings consistent beauty to every slab with none of the maintenance demands of natural stone.
