Ocean Grey

Actual Size Varies

Ocean Grey Marble is a high-quality Turkish marble from the Sivas region, known for its homogeneous structure and excellent polishing capabilities. With a density of approximately 2.75 and substantial hardness, this stone stands up beautifully to everyday use while retaining its refined aesthetic. Ocean Grey's consistent coloring and ability to take on deep polish make it a versatile selection for flooring, walls, counters, and architectural elements. Natural stone's inherent durability and timeless beauty allow it to serve both modern and classic design schemes. Bring lasting elegance into your space with Ocean Grey—experience the strength and beauty of one of Turkey's finest marbles.
