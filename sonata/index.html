Sonata

PP681 · Engineered Quartz by Empirestone

Sonata by Empirestone brings a refined harmony of soft white and cream tones to any interior. Its delicate, barely-there veining creates a serene surface that adapts beautifully to both modern minimalist and transitional design schemes. As an engineered quartz surface, Sonata offers exceptional durability, scratch resistance, and requires no sealing — making it a practical choice for high-use areas without sacrificing elegance. Ideal for kitchen countertops, bathroom vanities, and wall cladding where a clean, luminous backdrop is desired.
