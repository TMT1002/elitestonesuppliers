Misterio White

PQ115 · Engineered Quartz by Empirestone

Misterio White by Empirestone offers the pure, clean aesthetic of polished white stone with the engineered consistency that demanding projects require. Subtle surface movement adds depth without distraction, making it the ideal choice for spaces where white is the design language — from sleek contemporary kitchens to bright, airy bathrooms. As an engineered quartz, Misterio White is non-porous, scratch-resistant, and completely maintenance-free, delivering lasting whiteness without the staining risk of natural white marble.
