Sebaste Flower

88×82 & 79×80 • Actual Size Varies (Slab, Tile & Custom Cut to Size)

Sebaste Flower Marble is a premium Turkish marble quarried in the geological heartland of Anatolia, celebrated for its graceful vein patterns and soft neutral tones. This versatile marble embodies timeless beauty that enhances both contemporary and classic interior spaces. Its natural polish brings out depth and character in each slab, while honed options offer a subtle matte aesthetic. Ideal for floors, walls, countertops, and architectural features, Sebaste Flower combines natural elegance with the durability homeowners and designers expect from Turkish marble. Natural stone's inherent strength, resistance to wear, and ability to develop character over time make this marble an exceptional choice for kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas. Bring the artistry of Turkey's rich stone heritage into your home with Sebaste Flower—experience its calming presence and texture today.
