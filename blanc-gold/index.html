Blanc Gold

PQ197 · Engineered Quartz by Empirestone

Blanc Gold by Empirestone is a statement of pure luxury — a brilliant white quartz surface illuminated by luminous gold veining that moves across the slab like threads of precious metal. This surface brings an unmistakable sense of opulence and refinement to kitchen islands, master bathroom vanities, and feature wall installations. Engineered for consistency and durability, Blanc Gold delivers the drama of luxurious stone with none of the maintenance complexity — a truly special surface for elevated residential and boutique hospitality projects.
