Sebaste Moire

Premium Turkish Marble

Sebaste Moire is distinguished by its striking moire-like veining pattern that creates unique depth and visual movement. Quarried at 2,600 meters altitude in Sivas, Türkiye, this marble offers exceptional abrasion resistance, scratch resistance, and zero salt oxidation — preferred for high-traffic commercial projects and luxury residential interiors where durability and beauty are both essential.
