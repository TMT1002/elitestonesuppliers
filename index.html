About Us

Elite Stone Suppliers LLC.

International leader in natural stone since 2008

An international company and regional leader in the natural stone industry. Since its establishment in 2008, the company has expanded significantly, becoming a global model in the natural stone sector through innovation and technological leadership.

Core strengths include responsiveness, competitiveness, and continuous innovation. Located in Sivas, Türkiye, ESS Marble operates with advanced production technologies to serve global markets with high-quality marble.

History

Since 2008, ESS has extracted natural marble from quarries at approximately 2,500 meters altitude in Sivas, Türkiye. The company exports block and slab marble globally and is used in major international construction projects.

The quarry contributes to regional economic development, employment, and sustainable business partnerships.

▲

Geographic Location & Reserve

  • Elevation: ~2,550 m
  • Temp. resistance: up to −60°C
  • Total area: 300 hectares
  • Depth: 1,000–1,200 m
  • Estimated reserve: ~40 billion tons
  • Color variations: 24+
◆

Marble Structure & Geology

  • Density: ~2.7 g/cm³
  • Hardness: 3.65 Mohs
  • Structure: Compact, void-free limestone
  • Suitable for block and slab production with high durability and polish capability.

Environmental Commitment

ESS uses certified materials and eco-friendly processes to ensure sustainability, safe indoor environments, and long-lasting product performance.
